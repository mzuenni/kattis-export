        \begin{column}[T]{.62\textwidth}
            \begin{itemize}
                \item There is a hidden function $f\colon \{1,2,\ldots,n\} \to \{1,2,\ldots,n\}$, where~$n \leq \maxn$.
                \item You may ask at most $\maxq$ times for the value of $f^c(r) = \underbrace{f(\cdots f(}_{c~\mathrm{times}}r)\cdots)$ for $c$ and $r$ in $\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$.
                \item Find values of $c$ and $r$ such that $f^c(r) = c$.
                \item If there are multiple valid solutions, you may output any one of them.
                \item The interactor is not adaptive: the hidden function $f$ is fixed up front, and does not depend on your queries.
                \item A testing tool is provided to help you develop your solution.
            \end{itemize}
