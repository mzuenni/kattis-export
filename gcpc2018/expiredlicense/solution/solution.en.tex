\begin{slide}
    \begin{block}{Problem}
        Given two floating point numbers representing the aspect ratio $a \times b$ of a photo, find prime numbers $p$ and $q$ such that $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{p}{q}$.
    \end{block}
    \pause
    \begin{block}{Solution}
        \begin{itemize}
        	\item Multiply $a$ and $b$ by $10^5$ to make both numbers integral.
        	\item Divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor.
          \item Check whether the resulting two numbers are prime, e.g. using the Sieve of Eratosthenes.
        	\item Special case: quadratic aspect ratios, i.e. $6 \times 6$.
          \item Take care of numerical issues when using floating point arithmetics, e.g. $0.00007 \cdot 10^5 = 6.9999999999999991118\ldots$
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
\end{slide}
