Building

	Suppose you are building a house out of bricks.
	Each wall consists of k * k bricks and you want your house to be in the shape of a regular n-agon.

	You also want your house to be unique, so you color every brick a different color (out of l available colors).
	One day, you notice that all your neightbours have started building similar houses as well, only with different colors.

	Since you desperately want your house to be unique, you wonder how many possible colorings there are (not including rotation).

Solution

	Application of Burnside's Lemma.
	Very similar to the necklace problem, just with k*k blocks instead of beads.
	(Reflections could be included, but may not make sense with houses :) )
