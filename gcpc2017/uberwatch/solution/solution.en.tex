\begin{slide}
    \begin{block}{Problem}
        A game of Überwatch is played over $n$ time slices. The player can defeat all currently visible opponents. The attack must be charged for $m$ time slices after every use and at the beginning of the game.
        What is the maximum number of opponents that can be defeated?
    \end{block}
    \begin{block}{Observations}
        The greedy strategy of firing as fast as possible might skip a time slice needed for the optimal solution. $\rightarrow$ \textcolor{red}{WA}
        
        Only two choices in every time slice: 
        \begin{itemize}
        \item Fire \\ Must not have fired for atleast $m$ time slices. Score current number of opponents.
        \item Wait \\ Score does not change.
        \end{itemize}        
    \end{block}
\end{slide}


\begin{slide}
    \begin{block}{Problem}
        A game of Überwatch is played over $n$ time slices. The player can defeat all currently visible opponents. The attack must be charged for $m$ time slices after every use and at the beginning of the game.
        What is the maximum number of opponents that can be defeated?
    \end{block}
    \begin{block}{Solution} 
        Solution for time slice $t$:
        \[solution(t) = \max(\underbrace{opponents(t) + solution(t-m)}_{\text{Fire}}, \underbrace{solution(t-1)}_{\text{Wait}})\]
        One dimensional dynamic programming to get $O(n)$ run time.\\
        Special case the intial charging.
    \end{block}
\end{slide}
