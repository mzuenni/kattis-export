\begin{slide}
  \begin{block}{Problem}
    Given a set $S$ of at most 18 words and a rectangular grid, fill the grid
    with letters such that every word occurs in the grid.
  \end{block}
  \pause
  \begin{block}{Solution}
    Use dynamic programming: for $S' \subseteq S$ and $s \in S'$ let
    \begin{align*}
      f(S',s) = & \text{ the earliest possible end position (row,column) of a text} \\
                & \text{ containing all the words from $S'$ and ending in $s$.}
    \end{align*}
    \vspace{-0.5cm}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item A solution exists if and only if one of the positions $f(S,s)$
        still lies in the grid.
      \item To reconstruct the solution one needs to keep track of the best predecessor
        states while building the DP table (see next slide).
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
\end{slide}
\begin{slide}
  \begin{block}{Solution}
    \vspace{-0.5cm} 
    \begin{align*}
      f(S',s) = & \text{ the earliest possible end position (row,column) of a text} \\
                & \text{ containing all the words from $S'$ and ending in $s$.}
    \end{align*}

    The DP table can be constructed as follows:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item From the current state $(S',s)$ try to append every $t \notin S'$
        to the text, maximizing overlap between $s$ and $t$. \\
        If $t$ doesn't fit in the current line, start a new line. \\
        The new state is $(S' \cup \{t\},t)$.
      \item The maximal overlaps between words can be precalculated.
        Possible pitfall: some words may be substrings of other words.
      \item Time complexity: $\mathcal{O}(n^2 2^n)$.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
\end{slide}
