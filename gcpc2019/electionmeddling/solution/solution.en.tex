\begin{slide}
  \begin{block}{Problem}
    Given an election with voting districts and majority rule, how many voters have to be bribed such
    that our party wins a majority of districts?
  \end{block}
  \pause
  \begin{block}{Solution}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item For each district simulate how many votes are needed to achieve majority by taking one 
        vote at a time from the currently highest voted party and adding it to our total.
      \item Greedily take the districts that need the fewest votes until our party has won the majority
        of districts.
      \pause
      \item Can be sped up by taking enough votes at a time from the currently highest voted parties until they are equal
        to the next highest party. Repeat until our party has the majority of votes.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
\end{slide}
