\problemname{Upside down primes}

\noindent Last night, I must have dropped my alarm clock. When the alarm went
off in the morning, it showed {\tt 51:80} instead of {\tt 08:15}. This made me
realize that if you rotate a seven segment display like it is used in digital
clocks by 180 degrees, some numbers still are numbers after turning them upside
down.\\

\noindent My favourite numbers are primes, of course. Your job is to check
whether a number is a prime and still a prime when turned upside down.

\section*{Input}
One line with the integer $N$ in question ($1 \le N \le 10^{16}$). $N$
will not have leading zeros.

\section*{Output}
Print one line of output containing
``{\tt yes}'' if the number is a prime and still a prime if turned upside down,
``{\tt no}'' otherwise.
