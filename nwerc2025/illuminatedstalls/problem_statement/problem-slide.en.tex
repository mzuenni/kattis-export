    \begin{columns}
        \begin{column}[T]{.70\textwidth}
            \begin{itemize}
                \item Given $n \leq 2 \cdot 10^5$ axis-aligned segments in 2D (neon light tubes).
                \item Horizontal lights do not touch other horizontal lights, same for vertical.
                \item Can you move/rotate one light to form a square?
                \item Up to $20\,000$ test cases per run, with at most $2\cdot 10^5$ tubes in total.
            \end{itemize}
